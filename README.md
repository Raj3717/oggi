# Oggi (Off Grid Generic Intelligence)

A private AI assistant that works with you, wherever you are, even offline.

## What is Oggi?

Oggi is a local AI assistant inspired by the idea of a personal "Jarvis" built to run entirely on your own hardware.

Unlike cloud-first assistants, Oggi prioritizes:

- 🔒 Privacy
- ⚡ Low latency
- 🌐 Offline capability
- 🧠 Local reasoning
- 🛠 Tool usage
- 📚 Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Your documents stay on your computer.
Your conversations stay on your computer.
Your models stay on your computer.
